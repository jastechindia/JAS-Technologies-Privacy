# JASPay – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** Nov 15, 2026  
**Application:** JASPay  
**Version:** 0.8.1  
**Developer:** JAS Technologies India

## 1. Introduction

JASPay is an Android application designed to help merchants generate UPI payment QR codes and maintain collection totals for each configured UPI ID.

This Privacy Policy explains what information JASPay stores, how it is used, how it is handled, and the choices available to users.

## 2. Information stored on your device

JASPay stores information locally on your Android device to provide its features.

The information stored may include:

**A. UPI ID configuration**
- UPI IDs configured by the user
- Nicknames assigned to UPI IDs
- Associated bank names
- Date of creation and active/paused status
- Payment limits and other configuration details
- Associated UPI QR information, where available
- Collection counts and totals associated with each UPI ID

**B. Payment and collection information**
- Payment amount
- Payment date and time
- Associated UPI ID
- Payment status, such as pending, cancelled, confirmed or refunded
- Confirmation information, where applicable

Individual payment entries are maintained locally to calculate and display collection totals for each configured UPI ID.

**C. Business profile**
- Business name entered by the user
- Business logo, if provided

This information is stored in the application's local database. It is used to provide payment QR generation, collection accounting, reports and related app functionality.

## 3. QR code scanning

JASPay uses Google's Code Scanner through Google Play services to scan QR codes.

QR scanning is used to read UPI QR information and populate relevant details within the application.

The scanner is provided by Google. Its processing and any associated technical or diagnostic data handling are subject to Google's applicable privacy disclosures and terms.

JASPay does not operate its own server for processing scanned QR information.

## 4. Data sharing and transmission

JASPay does not operate a developer-managed backend server. The UPI configuration, payment and collection information, and business profile stored by JASPay are maintained locally on the device and are not uploaded by JASPay to a JASPay-operated server.

JASPay uses Google's Code Scanner dependency for QR scanning. Google Play services may process technical or diagnostic information in connection with that functionality, subject to Google's applicable privacy disclosures.

Users may choose to share generated payment QR images and associated payment request information through Android's system sharing interface. The information is then shared with the application or service selected by the user. The receiving application or service may handle that information according to its own privacy policy.

JASPay does not include advertising or a third-party payment gateway in the current version.

## 5. Backup and restore

JASPay provides an export and import facility to help users transfer their app data between devices.

- Backup files are generated locally by the application.
- The user selects the backup destination through Android's system file picker.
- Import is performed using a backup file selected by the user.
- JASPay does not upload exported backup files to a JASPay-operated server.

Backup files may contain UPI configuration, payment and collection information, and business profile information, including the business logo if provided.

Users are responsible for choosing a secure backup location and protecting exported backup files from unauthorized access.

Deleting JASPay does not necessarily delete backup files previously exported to other storage locations.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

JASPay retains locally stored information until it is deleted through Android's application storage controls or the application is uninstalled.

The current version of JASPay does not provide an in-app option to delete all stored data.

Users can delete JASPay's locally stored application data by either:

- Uninstalling JASPay; or
- Going to Android Settings → Apps → JASPay → Storage and selecting **Clear storage** or **Clear data** (the exact wording may vary by device).

Clearing the application cache alone does not delete its stored database.

Exported backup files are separate from the application's internal storage and must be deleted separately by the user from their respective storage locations.

## 7. Data security

JASPay stores its application data in Android's application-specific storage, which is protected by Android's application security mechanisms.

Users should keep their devices secure and take appropriate precautions when exporting, storing or sharing backup files, as these files may contain financial and business-related information.

## 8. Children's privacy

JASPay is intended for business and merchant use and is not designed specifically for children.
